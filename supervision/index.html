Before you book

  • Send any draft, problem set, or code you would like to discuss at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Write two or three specific questions in the booking notes — it makes the half hour go much further.
  • Need longer than 30 minutes? Book one slot and say so in the notes, and I will extend it where my diary allows.
  • Please cancel or reschedule through the confirmation email if your plans change, so the slot frees up for someone else.

What I supervise

Undergraduate and MPhil theses in industrial organization, financial, development, behavioural, and environmental economics, as well as coursework and empirical-methods questions arising from my teaching.

I supervise MPhil students in the Department of Economics and undergraduate and master's theses in industrial organisation, financial, development, behavioural and environmental economics.

Before you book

  • Send any draft, problem set or code you want to discuss at least 24 hours in advance.
  • Put two or three specific questions in the booking notes. The half hour goes much further.
  • Need longer? Book one slot and say so in the notes; I extend it where the diary allows.
  • If your plans change, cancel or reschedule through the confirmation email so the slot frees up for someone else.

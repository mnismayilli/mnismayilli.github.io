## 1. The thing that is missing from the plan

The plan so far is "pass SQE1 and SQE2." For most candidates that is only two thirds of
qualification. The third part is **two years of qualifying work experience (QWE)** — supervised
legal work signed off by a solicitor or COLP.

Foreign **qualified lawyers** are excused QWE. The SRA's own wording is that they "do not need to do
qualifying work experience, as we will recognise your existing qualification and experience."

But the SRA defines a qualified lawyer as someone holding "a legal professional qualification that
we recognise **which confers rights to practise**." A law degree on its own, without practising
rights and without practice, does not obviously meet that. On the current facts — Russian law
degree, no advokat status, not practising as a lawyer — the honest reading is that **she is
probably not a qualified lawyer for SRA purposes and will need the full two years of QWE.**

That is a bigger obstacle than either exam, and it is currently unbudgeted for.

**Action 1 (do this first, costs nothing): email the SRA and ask the single question —**
> "I hold a Russian law degree but not advokat status and have not practised as a lawyer.
> Do I meet the definition of a qualified lawyer under the Qualified Lawyers Regulations,
> and am I therefore exempt from qualifying work experience?"

Nothing else in this plan should be finalised until that answer is in writing. QWE can be
accumulated before, during or after the exams, so if it is required, arranging it starts now
and runs in parallel with study — it does not queue behind the exams.

## 2. The SQE2 exemption is not available

Worth stating so it is not revisited. An SQE2 exemption requires:

- practising rights in at least one of: criminal litigation, civil litigation, property practice,
  wills and probate, business organisations; **and**
- a minimum of two years' professional legal work experience; **and**
- competence at Level 3 of the SRA Threshold Standard (day-one solicitor).

Fee £265, decision takes up to 180 days. On the current facts none of the three limbs is met.
Do not spend the £265. **Plan for sitting both SQE1 and SQE2.**

## 4. What SQE1 actually is

Two papers, 180 single-best-answer multiple choice questions each, 360 total. Roughly 1.7 minutes
per question. It tests **application** of law to a client scenario, not recall of doctrine.

**FLK1** — Business Law and Practice; Dispute Resolution; Contract; Tort; Legal System of England
and Wales, Constitutional and Administrative Law and EU Law; Legal Services.

**FLK2** — Property Practice; Wills and Administration of Estates; Solicitors Accounts; Land Law;
Trusts; Criminal Liability; Criminal Law and Practice.

Questions can combine subjects the way they would arise in practice, so siloed revision is a trap.

## 5. Why daily "topic + explanation" emails would not work

Reading an explanation produces recognition — the feeling of understanding — and very little
retrieval strength. It also trains none of the timing pressure that decides this exam.

The daily material must lead with a question she answers cold, before she sees any explanation,
with the ones she gets wrong resurfacing days and weeks later. That is the whole design.

There is a second reason: an AI writing English law from memory will produce statements that are
subtly wrong, and she has no way to detect them. Every question she drills must come from the
SRA specification and a professionally written question bank, not from generated content.

## 6. Purchases to make once the QWE question is answered

- A licensed SQE1 question bank — the highest-value single purchase. Several thousand questions,
  filterable by topic, with performance analytics.
- A revision guide series covering all 13 subjects (BPP, Revise SQE / Routledge, or ULaw manuals).
  Several hundred pounds for a full set.
- Full mock papers under exam conditions, from at least two different providers.

Treat vendor pass-rate claims sceptically. "Students who did 25+ mocks passed at 94%" measures
who was committed enough to do 25 mocks, not what the mocks did for them.
